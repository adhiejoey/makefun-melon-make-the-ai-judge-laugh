A one-line office joke for the "Melon: Make the AI Judge Laugh" bounty
(`u-rama-melon-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-302d0825`, $2 prize).

Running `node main.ts` writes a single original joke about office life to
`entry.txt` in the output directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`). The entry is one line, plain ASCII, no trailing
newline, and well under the 200-character limit.

The joke plays on the familiar boss line "we're a family here" and turns it
with an unexpected last word: asking for an inheritance — the raise promised
two performance reviews ago.
